About the Experience
This is the Arctic very few people reach

There are places on earth where very few people ever go. The remote wilderness of the Canadian Arctic is one of them. This trip is built around one of nature's most extraordinary seasonal moments: polar bears moving through fields of summer fireweed.

The journey begins in Winnipeg, with flights north to Churchill and onward transfers into a remote wilderness camp. From there, your days are shaped by weather, tides, light and wildlife movement. Each field session is about patience, respect and being ready when the Arctic gives you the moment.

Martin leads you into locations he has spent years quietly finding and returning to. You do not need to be a photographer to be here, but if you are, or if you want to become one, the access and instruction are unlike anything available on a standard wildlife tour.

Evenings at camp are for shared meals, stories, field notes, image reviews and the particular kind of conversation that only happens when a small group of people are somewhere genuinely remote together.

Your Storyteller Guide
Martin Gregus

Martin Gregus grew up in Bratislava, Slovakia, in a household full of artists. He learned photography from his father and was exploring local wildlife with a camera before most kids had decided what they wanted to do with their lives.

Nineteen years later, his work has taken him across all seven continents and some of the most remote ecosystems on the planet. He has earned two wins and two nominations at Wildlife Photographer of the Year, and his 2022 polar bear project, 33 Days Among the Bears, became an international story after National Geographic coverage.

His cinematography has contributed to productions for Disney, BBC and Netflix. Now based in Canada, Martin is recognised internationally for his work in polar regions, remote wilderness and the patience required to create images that feel impossible until you see them.

Why Book This Experience?
A field expedition, not a viewing platform

Most people will only ever see polar bears from a crowded vehicle, a distant ship deck or a documentary. This expedition puts you in the field with Martin, working from a remote camp and moving at the pace of the Arctic.

The difference is access, but access alone is not enough. Martin's approach is grounded in patience and preparation. He teaches you how to read behaviour, work with distance, compose in difficult light and respect the animal first.

This trip also keeps the group deliberately tiny. Six guests means quieter movement, more direct instruction and a field rhythm that can adapt to conditions. When a bear steps through the fireweed, you are not fighting a crowd for the same frame.

The result is a rare blend of wilderness, conservation, photography and genuine time with someone who has devoted years to understanding this landscape.

FAQ
Everything You Need to Know
Is it safe to be around polar bears?
Polar bears are apex predators, and your safety depends on following Martin, your Airguides expedition leader and the local field team's guidance at all times. Safe distances are strictly maintained to protect both guests and the bears. This trip is built around observation, not baiting, feeding or disrupting natural behaviour.
Do I need photography experience?
No. This trip is powerful for wildlife lovers as well as photographers. Martin teaches in the field and at camp, so beginners can learn the fundamentals while experienced photographers can work on behaviour, composition, editing and storytelling.
Do I need a visa?
Many nationalities are visa-exempt for Canada and can enter with an eTA, Electronic Travel Authorisation. Check the Canadian immigration website for your nationality, or ask the Airguides team for help.
What is the drone policy?
Experienced operators may fly from camp only, and only when no guests or staff are in the field. You need a valid drone licence from your home country and liability insurance of up to US$1,000,000. Staff make the final call on where and when flying is permitted.
Is there a solo traveller supplement?
Yes. Solo travellers can request a private room for an additional fee. Roommate pairing may be available on a limited, first-come-first-served basis. Contact Airguides for current availability.
Can I get help with flights?
Yes. The Airguides team can help with flight options and travel routing. Call, text or WhatsApp (+61) 402 552 918 to speak with someone who knows the trip.
