The Story

This is not polished safari theatre.

The Congo Basin is older, denser and far less travelled than the safari landscapes most people know. Wildlife is not lined up on open plains here. It is hidden in green shadow, heard before it is seen, and revealed slowly to travellers willing to move at the forest's pace.

This expedition follows Kamba's classic Odzala route, moving through Ngaga, Lango and Mboko lodges. Each camp reveals a different side of the forest, from closed-canopy gorilla habitat to bai, river systems, savanna and rainforest edge.

With Martin Gregus beside you, the journey becomes more than rare wildlife access. It is a chance to learn how to observe patiently, work with low light and dense forest, and create images in one of the most complex ecosystems on the planet.

Your Storyteller

Martin Gregus knows how to wait for the frame.

Martin has worked across all seven continents, from polar ice to dense wilderness. His work has earned two Wildlife Photographer of the Year wins, National Geographic coverage and cinematography credits across Disney, BBC and Netflix productions.

In the Congo, that experience matters. Low light, dense canopy and quick wildlife encounters reward people who understand patience, preparation and restraint.

Martin's role is not to turn the forest into a classroom. It is to help you notice more, move better and come home with images that carry the feeling of being there.

Day By Day

The route through Odzala-Kokoua

Day
1

Brazzaville to Ngaga Lodge

Begin with Kamba airport assistance and the scheduled charter flight to Odzala. From Mboko Airstrip, transfer by open 4x4 through savanna and rainforest to Ngaga Lodge, then meet the guiding team for a conservation and gorilla tracking briefing.

Days
2-4

Gorilla Tracking at Ngaga

Three western lowland gorilla permits are included. Tracks are led by expert guides and trackers through closed-canopy forest. Between tracks, join ecology walks, nocturnal safaris, night drives and photography sessions with Martin.

Day
5

Ngaga to Lango Bai

After breakfast and a final forest morning, travel through Odzala-Kokoua National Park toward the waterways. Continue by kayak along the Lekoli River, watching for monkeys, forest buffalo, elephants and birdlife before arriving at Lango Lodge.

Days
6-7

Lango Bai

Explore Lango's wetlands, bai and surrounding forest on foot and by water. Walk elephant trails through streams and forest clearings, watch for forest buffalo, bongo and shy forest elephants, and listen for chimps in the distance.

Day
8

Lango to Mboko

Begin with an early walk before transferring to Mboko Lodge, where forest, savanna and river meet. After lunch, enjoy a forest walk, time on the river deck or a late afternoon river cruise.

Days
9-10

Rivers, Forest and Savanna

Walk, boat and kayak through some of Odzala's most varied habitats. Search for birdlife, forest elephants, buffalo, monkeys, spotted hyenas, bongo and hippos, then return to quiet evenings by the Lekeni River.

Day
11

Return to Brazzaville

Final breakfast at Mboko, then a game drive back through the savanna to the airstrip for your Kamba charter flight to Brazzaville. The team coordinates your return so the logistics feel calm.
