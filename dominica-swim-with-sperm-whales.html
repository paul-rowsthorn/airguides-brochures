About the Experience
Meet the largest toothed predators on the planet, on their terms

Dominica is one of the few places on Earth where sperm whales can be encountered in warm, clear Caribbean water.

Each morning, you will head out with Karim, a local captain and an experienced crew who know these waters and how to read whale behaviour. The search is part of the experience: scanning for blows, listening for clicks and waiting for the right moment.

When conditions and the whales allow, you will slip into the water quietly. You may encounter mothers and calves near the surface, social groups moving through the blue, or whales communicating through the clicks and codas that carry across the ocean.

Nothing is staged and no sighting is guaranteed. That honesty is what makes a real encounter so powerful.

Every day is different. Some moments are brief. Others stay with you for life.

Your Storyteller Guide
Karim Iliya

Karim Iliya

Wildlife Photographer, Filmmaker and Conservationist

Karim's work offers a rare glimpse into the lives of some of Earth's most compelling species. From swimming with whales to filming volcanic eruptions from above, his images have appeared in National Geographic, BBC Earth and leading global publications.

He brings more than technical mastery. His approach is grounded in patience, instinct and a deep respect for the natural world. In Dominica, he is beside you in the water and behind the lens, helping you understand behaviour, improve your underwater photography and stay present for the encounter itself.

"Photography lets me see the Earth in a new way, where I take my time looking at the details that you don't usually see." - Karim Iliya
Why This Trip?
Time, patience and the right people on the water

Wildlife does not work to a timetable. That is why this expedition gives you five full days at sea instead of reducing the experience to one rushed attempt.

The local captain and crew bring deep knowledge of Dominica's waters. Karim brings years of experience around whales and a thoughtful approach to underwater storytelling. The six-guest limit keeps every day personal.

The goal is not to chase an image at any cost. It is to understand what you are seeing, move with respect and be ready when a rare, natural encounter unfolds.

FAQ
What guests usually need to know
Are sperm whale encounters guaranteed?
No. These are wild animals and every day at sea is different. Sightings and in-water encounters cannot be scripted or guaranteed, and entering the water always depends on conditions and whale behaviour. Five full days at sea with experienced local captains, crew and Karim give you the strongest possible chance of a respectful, natural encounter.
Do I need to be a photographer?
No. The photography and filmmaking guidance is optional. Some guests come to improve their images, while others come purely for the experience of being in the water with whales.
Do I need scuba certification?
No. Encounters are by snorkelling and, when appropriate, optional freediving. You should be a confident swimmer and comfortable snorkelling in open water.
What is the group size?
The expedition is limited to six guests. The small group keeps boat days personal and supports calm, respectful encounters.
How do I get to Dominica?
Fly into Douglas Charles Airport, airport code DOM. Airport pickup and drop-off are included, and the Airguides team can help with flight routing before you book.
